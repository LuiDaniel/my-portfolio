I'm Luis Daniel Quecara Machaca, a full stack developer from Juliaca, Peru, and a Systems Engineering student at Universidad Peruana Unión. I like building complete products: understanding the problem, designing the data, writing the API and polishing every detail of the interface.

## How I started

I first trained at SENATI, where I completed five semesters and learned Java, databases and web application development. That's where I built my first projects: a console application for a transport company and a sales system with JavaFX.

In 2023 I worked as a backend developer at Sumak Desarrollo de Software y Marketing, with Go, Fiber and MariaDB stored procedures. Then I built, on my own and end to end, a warehouse system for a construction company with Django.

## What I do now

I study Systems Engineering, I take part in Vyra (a system for a dental clinic that we're building as a team) and I build projects with TypeScript and Next.js, such as the DaeTrym website and this portfolio.

## What drives me

I'm passionate about everything related to technology: software development, networking and cybersecurity. I learn by building real things and I like understanding how every layer works, from the server to the interface.
