## Context and problem

I wanted a portfolio that shows engineering judgment and attention to detail at the same time, without getting in the way of what recruiters actually need: finding the role, the projects and the contact details quickly.

## My role

The whole project: interface design, modeling and animating the robot, integrating it into the site, bilingual content and quality (tests, accessibility and performance).

## Solution

A short intro where a robot assembles the welcome card and says goodbye, followed by an Apple-style Home with bento cards. The robot only exists in the intro: the rest of the site is server-rendered HTML, fast and accessible.

## Architecture

- **Blender by script:** every robot part (head, torso, arms, legs, details) and the rig are generated by reproducible Python scripts, and the animations are exported as glTF clips.
- **Export:** parts are merged by bone and material and compressed with Draco to about 0.8 MB.
- **Web:** Next.js App Router and TypeScript; plain Three.js inside a single client component for the intro; JSON and MDX content validated with Velite; Spanish and English with next-intl.

## Technical decisions

- Plain Three.js instead of React Three Fiber, to reuse the prototype code and choreograph the intro with GSAP over time.
- Animations are made in Blender, not in the browser: the site only plays clips and reacts to their events.
- All content lives in `content/` and is validated at build time, so a data mistake breaks the build instead of the live site.

## Challenges

Syncing the Blender clip events (grab, release, sweep) with the welcome card DOM, and keeping the intro from hurting the Home's performance.

## Results

A bilingual site with the 3D intro, static pages and automated tests on every change. In the production build, mobile Lighthouse scores 100 for accessibility and between 90 and 100 for performance.

## What I'd do differently

I'd measure the Home's performance earlier: the role's entrance animation delays the LCP (3.6 s on mobile, above the 2.5 s target), and it's the next thing I'm going to optimize.
