## Why scripts

Every robot part is generated by a Python script in Blender. That makes the model reproducible: changing a measurement and running it again is faster than editing by hand.

## From rig to clips

The rig has one bone per rigid part, and the animations (wake up, look around, wave and the intro) are stored as actions exported as glTF clips.

## A light export

Parts are merged by bone and material and the result is compressed with Draco to about 0.8 MB.

## On the web

Three.js loads the model, plays the clips and listens to their events to keep the interface in sync.
